Why Rollout ≠ Raw Attention

Raw attention only shows the last layer's weights. Rollout multiplies attention across all 6 DistilBERT layers and adds identity matrices to account for residual skip connections. Tokens that receive attention in early layers but not the last layer will be invisible in raw attention but visible in rollout.

Why LRP > Raw Attention

LRP uses gradients to weight attention, keeping only positive relevance flow (via ReLU). Unlike raw attention, LRP is class-discriminative — it shows which tokens push the prediction toward the specific class, filtering out tokens the model looked at but didn't use for the decision.

Why IG Solves the "Sponge" Effect

While Rollout and LRP trace the internal attention mechanism (which gets messy because Transformers use periods and structural tokens as arbitrary memory bufers), IG bypasses attention entirely. It measures the physical gradient of the Input Word Embeddings as they fade from 0% to 100%. If destroying the input pixel changes the score, it highlights it. This perfectly isolates semantic meaning and naturally zeroes out structural punctuation.
